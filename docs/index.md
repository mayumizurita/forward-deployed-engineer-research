# Forward Deployed Engineer research

<p class="research-meta">7 job descriptions · 5 employers · Internships, new graduate and experienced roles · November 12, 2026</p>

**An FDE builds and deploys software alongside customers.** The work connects an unclear operational problem to a working system, then improves it using feedback from real users. This is a synthesis of the [postings in this sample](./jobs/index.md), not a universal job definition.

## Three findings to keep in mind

- **Coding and customer work appear together.** Pace asks interns to build workflows and work directly with customers; OpenAI expects full-stack delivery and adoption work. [Pace](./jobs/pace-intern.md) · [OpenAI](./jobs/openai.md)
- **The title covers different technical work.** Revel involves hardware integration; Netic focuses on AI agents; Palantir includes data, custom applications and AI workflows. [Revel](./jobs/revel-intern.md) · [Netic](./jobs/netic.md) · [Palantir](./jobs/palantir-grad.md)
- **Full-time does not mean entry-level.** Palantir has a graduation-specific role; OpenAI asks for 5+ years and Netic for 2+ years. [Compare full-time jobs](./full-time.md)

## Scope

US-focused, purposive sample: four internships, one new-graduate role and two experienced roles. It supports comparison, not estimates of the entire market. Descriptions were found on employer career pages or employer-hosted applicant systems. Availability is not guaranteed; see [source limitations](./sources.md).
