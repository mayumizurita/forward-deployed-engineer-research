# What the work involves

**The recurring pattern is discovery → build → deploy → improve.** The sequence below is an interpretation of the job descriptions; employers may divide the work differently.

| Stage | Concrete work in the postings | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Understand | Learn the customer's workflow and define a useful problem | [Pace](./jobs/pace-intern.md), [Netic](./jobs/netic.md) |
| Design & build | Write applications, integrations or AI workflows | [Palantir](./jobs/palantir-grad.md), [AiStudio](./jobs/aistudio-intern.md) |
| Deploy | Connect software to customer systems and help users adopt it | [OpenAI](./jobs/openai.md), [Revel](./jobs/revel-intern.md) |
| Debug & evaluate | Investigate failures, assess model behavior or troubleshoot hardware | [Pace](./jobs/pace-intern.md), [Revel](./jobs/revel-intern.md) |
| Improve the product | Convert recurring customer needs into reusable capabilities | [Netic](./jobs/netic.md), [OpenAI](./jobs/openai.md) |

## How responsibilities change with level

**Intern:** contribute to customer projects while learning. AiStudio explicitly describes a defined workstream with senior support; Pace describes broader ownership. Mentoring arrangements vary. [AiStudio](./jobs/aistudio-intern.md) · [Pace](./jobs/pace-intern.md)

**New graduate:** Palantir describes customer-facing, end-to-end ownership, architecture decisions and stakeholder relationships. Its new-graduate role should not be treated as a promise about every employer's expectations. [Palantir](./jobs/palantir-grad.md)

**Experienced:** OpenAI describes scoping and sequencing multiple deployments, managing trade-offs and taking systems into stable production. Netic adds production reliability and monitoring expectations. [OpenAI](./jobs/openai.md) · [Netic](./jobs/netic.md)

## Questions the title does not answer

Ask the hiring team: How much time is coding versus customer meetings? Who owns production support? How much travel actually occurs? What mentoring is available? What gets reused in the core product? The reviewed descriptions do not consistently quantify these points.
