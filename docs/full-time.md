## What changes after internships

The experienced descriptions emphasize production judgment: reliability, scope decisions, customer coordination and ownership after launch. The new-graduate description still assigns substantial ownership, but uses a graduation window rather than a years-of-experience threshold. This comparison is a synthesis of the three linked sources.

## If you are graduating soon

Use the new-graduate posting as the closest eligibility comparison. Treat the experienced roles as examples of future responsibilities; do not combine all their specialized requirements into a single entry-level checklist.
