## How the research was assembled

Selected actual descriptions with an FDE/FDSE title and enough information to compare work and qualifications. Prioritized employer-controlled pages, avoided recruiter reposts and did not count duplicate locations as separate roles. Included hardware work to avoid treating every FDE as an AI-agent engineer.

Each brief extracts career stage, location, duties, required/preferred qualifications and eligibility constraints. “Not stated” means the reviewed text did not establish the fact. Broader explanations and preparation advice are labeled as synthesis or interpretation.

## Limits

- This is a small convenience sample, weighted toward US software and AI roles. It is not a market census or a ranking of employers.
- Search-index text can lag the live page. JavaScript-only and blocked pages limited direct verification. Application status was not independently confirmed.
- Palantir supplies both an internship and graduate example; that relationship should not be mistaken for an industry-wide career ladder.
- No inferred salaries, visa policies or hiring odds. Employer language describes expectations, not verified daily experience.

## Update procedure

Reopen each original description before applying. Record the new check date, changed eligibility and whether the page is still available. If a posting closes, label it historical rather than silently replacing the evidence.
