# OpenAI · Experienced FDE

**Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) – Seattle** · Hybrid full-time

[Original job description](https://openai.com/careers/forward-deployed-engineer-%28fde%29-seattle-seattle/) · P7 · Checked November 12, 2026 on the employer career page; application status can change.

## Work

Lead discovery, scoping, design, implementation and rollout of customer LLM systems. Coordinate multiple deployments, guide adoption, and feed evaluation results into product/model improvements.

## Qualifications

**Employer fit criteria:**

- 5+ years of engineering or technical deployment experience, including customer-facing work.
- Production frontend/backend coding in Python, JavaScript or comparable stacks.
- Built or deployed LLM/generative-model systems.
- Scope decisions, communication, risk detection and judgment under pressure.

## Logistics

Seattle; three office days per week; up to 50% travel. Posted base compensation: $185,000–$300,000 plus equity; individual offers vary. This is an experienced-role example.
